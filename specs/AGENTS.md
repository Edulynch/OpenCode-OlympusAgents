# Specifications and evidence

- Put active feature specs under `NNN-feature-name/` and follow the [Spec Kit constitution](../.specify/memory/constitution.md) and [numbering rules](README.md#numeración-de-nuevas-features). Keep requirements, design, implementation tasks, and evidence in their corresponding files rather than copying them into the public docs.
- Put proposals that are not approved for implementation in [`future/`](future/README.md). Keep retrospective records under `retrospective/<version>/`; their IDs do not affect active feature numbering.
- Cite the relevant commit or release and label what was observed, simulated, or not run. Do not backfill historical evidence or store credentials, raw runtime dumps, or temporary artifacts here.
